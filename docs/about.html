About LivestockBook

LivestockBook is an independent app built to make livestock record keeping simpler.

The idea is straightforward: keep your animals and records organised, log important events as they happen, and export the information you need when it comes time to share records, prepare for an audit, or deal with local record-keeping requirements.

I’m building LivestockBook for farmers and livestock keepers who want something practical and easy to use, without unnecessary complexity.

Because this is an independent project, there will always be things that can be improved. If you come across a bug, have an idea for a feature, or need the app to support information required in your country, I genuinely want to hear about it.

I’ve created a Facebook group where users can ask questions, suggest features and discuss the app. You can also get in touch with me directly by email.

About me

Hi, I’m Tom, the developer behind LivestockBook.

I design, build and maintain the app myself, so feedback from people actually using it has a direct impact on what gets improved and what I work on next.

My aim is to keep LivestockBook simple, useful and focused on the things livestock keepers actually need.

If you’re interested in collaborating, have a suggestion, or just want to get in touch, feel free to send me a message.
