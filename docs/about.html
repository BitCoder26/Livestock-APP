About LivestockBook

Welcome to LivestockBook, your solution to simplifying livestock record keeping. We are dedicated to helping farmers and livestock keepers worldwide maintain accurate animal records, ensuring compliance with local regulations and enhancing everyday farm efficiency.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an easy-to-use, reliable, and comprehensive tool that meets the unique needs of livestock keepers including farmers, smallholders, breeders, contractors, and organisations. We aim to streamline the process of recording and managing livestock activity, making it accessible and convenient for everyone.

Features

  • Full history for every animal
  • Movements, weights, treatments, health checks and more
  • Multiple farms, paddocks, and groups
  • Filter records by animal, type, and date range
  • PDF and spreadsheet exports with your farm details
  • Reports on livestock breakdowns and activity
  • Backup and restore
  • Works offline

Developer

LivestockBook was developed by Thomas Koukouris to provide a convenient way for livestock keepers worldwide to track their animals and records. Thomas is committed to continuous improvement and maintenance of the app, ensuring that LivestockBook remains available to all users for the foreseeable future.
