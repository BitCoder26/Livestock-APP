About LivestockBook

Welcome to LivestockBook, your solution to simplifying livestock record keeping. The app is used to help keep track of your livestock and accurately record animal events. Exports can then be used for audits or to help you stay legally compliant. I am dedicated to helping farmers and livestock keepers worldwide maintain accurate animal records, supporting compliance with local regulations and enhancing everyday farm efficiency.

This app is an independent project. Therefore, it may have bugs and room for improvements. That's why I have created the Facebook group, to discuss ideas and provide support. If you have any idea in mind, or if the regulations in your country require other information than what I already have, let me know either in the group or send me an email.
