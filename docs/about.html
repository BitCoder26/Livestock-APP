About LivestockBook

LivestockBook is designed to make everyday livestock record keeping simpler from a phone. The goal is to give farmers and livestock keepers one straightforward place to add records, organise animals, set up their farm structure, and prepare exports when needed.

Why it exists

Day-to-day record keeping can easily become scattered across notebooks, messages, and spreadsheets. LivestockBook brings that work into a cleaner mobile flow so important details stay easier to capture and easier to find later.

What the app helps with

The app focuses on the core tasks people return to most often: adding new records quickly, reviewing animal records in one place, setting up farms and paddocks clearly, and exporting information when paperwork or sharing is required.

Built for practical use

The product direction behind LivestockBook is intentionally simple. Instead of overcomplicating the workflow, it aims to reduce friction and keep common tasks fast, readable, and manageable while you are working through everyday livestock administration.

Current direction

LivestockBook is being shaped around real-world usage and feedback. The website highlights the current mobile experience, while ongoing updates continue to refine record entry, organisation, setup tools, and export flows.
