Add Record

Add records fast

Create health, weight, movement, sale, and count records in a guided flow that feels quick in the field. Key details stay easy to review before saving, so everyday record keeping takes less effort.

Animal Records

Keep animal records organised

Browse your animals in one clear register with the important details visible at a glance. It is easier to check status, identify the right animal, and keep your records current as your herd grows.

Setup

Set up your farm structure

Set up farms, paddocks, groups, and the supporting lists your records depend on before you get started. That structure keeps day-to-day entry cleaner and helps the app match how your operation is organised.

Exports

Export only what you need

Turn your stored records into clean exports whenever you need to share information or prepare paperwork. You can pull together the details you need without re-entering the same data somewhere else.
